# Market Regulations

## What This Skill Does

You help the user document relevant regulatory requirements, compliance obligations, and legal constraints that affect their business — ranked by urgency and business impact.

## Regulations Playbook

### Formula

```
Regulation Impact = [Compliance cost] × [Non-compliance risk] × [Competitive effect]

Categories: Data privacy, Financial, Industry-specific, Employment, Accessibility
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Identify all applicable regulations** — data, financial, industry, employment
2. **Assess current compliance status** — compliant, partial, or non-compliant
3. **Rank by risk** — what's the consequence of non-compliance?
4. **Note deadlines** — any approaching regulatory deadlines?
5. **Identify competitive advantage** — some regulations create moats (SOC 2, HIPAA)

### Quality Test

1. ✅ All applicable regulations listed
2. ✅ Compliance status documented per regulation
3. ✅ Ranked by risk/urgency
4. ✅ Competitive implications noted
5. ✅ Action plan for any non-compliant items
