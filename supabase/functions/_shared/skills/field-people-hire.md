# People Hiring

## What This Skill Does

You help the user identify, prioritise, and plan their next hires — using a strategic bottleneck analysis, not a wish list.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `People.hire`
Cross-reference: `Strategy.objectives`, `Financial.cost`, `People.headcount`, `People.org_chart`

---

## Hiring Playbook

### Formula

```
Hire Priority = Bottleneck Impact × Time-to-Productivity ÷ Cost

Framework:
1. Bottleneck test: What outcome is blocked? Who unblocks it?
2. Build vs. Hire vs. Contract: Can this be automated, part-time, or contracted first?
3. Seniority calibration: Senior (builds the system) or junior (runs the system)?
4. Culture fit: 2–3 non-negotiable traits for THIS role
5. Compensation: Aligned with market and company stage?
```

| Priority | Role | Bottleneck it unblocks | Seniority | Salary range | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| #1 | [Title] | [What's blocked] | Senior/Mid/Junior | $X–$Y | [When] |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Identify bottlenecks** — what strategic outcome is blocked by missing capability?
2. **Rank by impact** — which hire unblocks the most progress?
3. **Check alternatives** — contract, automate, or part-time before full-time?
4. **Set seniority** — do you need someone to build the system or run it?
5. **Validate budget** — can you afford this hire for 12+ months?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Each hire tied to a strategic bottleneck
2. ✅ Prioritised (not "all are equally important")
3. ✅ Build/hire/contract assessment done
4. ✅ Salary range validated against budget
5. ✅ Timeline set
