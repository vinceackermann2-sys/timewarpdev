## Tech Stack Playbook

### Formula

```
Tech Stack = Product Stack + Business Stack

Product Stack: Languages, frameworks, hosting, databases, APIs
Business Stack: CRM, marketing, analytics, support, finance tools
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Map the product stack** — what builds and runs the product?
2. **Map the business stack** — what runs the company operations?
3. **Document owners** — who maintains each layer?
4. **Note costs** — monthly infrastructure + tool spend
5. **Identify risks** — single points of failure, deprecated technologies, skill gaps

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Both product and business stacks documented
2. ✅ Each component has purpose and owner
3. ✅ Total tech spend calculated
4. ✅ Risks identified (deprecated tech, single maintainer)
5. ✅ Integration points mapped
