# Strategy Decision Framework

## What This Skill Does

You help the user define how the company makes important decisions — who decides, what criteria matter, and when to move fast vs. slow.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Strategy.decision_framework`
Cross-reference: `Strategy.risk_appetite`, `Strategy.bets`, `People.org_chart`

---

## Decision Framework Playbook

### Formula

```
Decision = [Type] × [Reversibility] × [Who decides] × [Criteria]

Bezos model:
- Two-way door (reversible): Move fast, decide with 70% information
- One-way door (irreversible): Slow down, get 90% information, involve senior leaders
```

| Decision type | Example | Reversibility | Who decides | Process |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product feature | Add a feature | Two-way door | Product lead | Decide fast, test |
| Pricing change | Raise prices 20% | One-way door | CEO + CFO | Data + customer input |
| Hiring | New VP hire | One-way door | CEO | Full process |
| Vendor | Switch CRM | Two-way door | Ops lead | Compare + migrate |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Categorise decision types** — product, pricing, hiring, strategic bets
2. **Assess reversibility** — one-way or two-way door?
3. **Assign decision makers** — who decides for each type?
4. **Define criteria** — what factors are weighted?
5. **Document the process** — who's consulted, who's informed?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Decision types categorised
2. ✅ Reversibility assessed per type
3. ✅ Decision makers named
4. ✅ Criteria documented
5. ✅ RACI or similar framework applied
