# Strategy Kill Signals

## What This Skill Does

You help the user pre-define the conditions under which a strategy, bet, or initiative is abandoned — removing emotion from shut-down decisions by committing to criteria in advance.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Strategy.kill_signals`
Cross-reference: `Strategy.bets`, `Strategy.objectives`, `Financial.forecast`, `Strategy.risk_appetite`

---

## Kill Signals Playbook

### Formula

```
Kill Signal = [Metric] + [Threshold] + [Timeframe] + [What happens when triggered]

Pre-commitment: Define kill signals BEFORE launching, not after sunk cost accumulates.
```

| Initiative | Kill metric | Threshold | Timeframe | Action when triggered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Bet/Initiative] | [Specific metric] | [Below X] | [By when] | [Stop/Pivot/Reduce] |

**Examples:**
- "If paid social CAC exceeds $200 after 90 days of optimisation, kill the channel"
- "If new product activation rate stays below 15% after 3 sprints, pivot the approach"
- "If enterprise pipeline < $500K after 6 months of sales effort, exit enterprise"

### How to Fill This Field

1. **For each active bet/initiative** — define what failure looks like
2. **Pick a leading metric** — the earliest signal that it's not working
3. **Set a threshold** — below what number is it not viable?
4. **Set a timeframe** — how long do you give it before evaluating?
5. **Pre-commit the action** — stop, pivot, or reduce resources

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Kill signal defined for every active bet
2. ✅ Each has a specific metric and threshold
3. ✅ Each has a timeframe
4. ✅ Action is pre-committed (not "we'll evaluate")
5. ✅ Team is aligned on the signals
