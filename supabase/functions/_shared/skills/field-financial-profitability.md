# Financial Profitability

## What This Skill Does

You help the user map their path to profitability — calculating break-even, identifying the levers, and building a timeline.

## Profitability Playbook

### Formula

```
Break-Even Revenue = Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin %
Break-Even Customers = Fixed Costs ÷ (ARPU × Gross Margin %)
Months to Break-Even = (Break-Even MRR − Current MRR) ÷ Net Monthly MRR Growth

Profitability Levers (in order of impact):
1. Reduce churn (improves LTV and MRR retention)
2. Increase ARPU (pricing/upsell)
3. Reduce CAC (channel optimisation)
4. Cut costs (last resort — growth > cuts at early stage)
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Calculate break-even revenue** — how much monthly revenue covers all costs?
2. **Calculate break-even customers** — how many paying customers at current ARPU?
3. **Calculate months to break-even** — at current growth rate
4. **Identify the biggest lever** — pricing, churn, or cost?
5. **Model scenarios** — what if you improve the #1 lever by 20%?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Break-even revenue calculated
2. ✅ Break-even customer count calculated
3. ✅ Timeline to break-even estimated
4. ✅ Top profitability lever identified
5. ✅ Scenario modelled with improvement to top lever
