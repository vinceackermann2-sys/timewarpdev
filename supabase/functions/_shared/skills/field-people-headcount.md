# People Headcount

## What This Skill Does

You help the user plan and manage headcount — current team size by function, planned growth, and the financial implications of each hire.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `People.headcount`
Cross-reference: `Financial.cost`, `Financial.revenue`, `Strategy.objectives`, `People.hire`

## Headcount Playbook

### Formula

```
Revenue per Employee = ARR ÷ Total headcount (SaaS benchmark: $150K–$300K)
Headcount Ratio = Function headcount ÷ Total headcount
People Cost Ratio = Total people cost ÷ Revenue (target: 60–80%)
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Count current headcount** by function
2. **Calculate revenue per employee** — are you efficient?
3. **Compare ratios to benchmarks** — Engineering typically 40–50% in SaaS
4. **Plan next 12 months** — which functions grow and by how much?
5. **Check financial feasibility** — can the business afford the planned hires?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Current headcount by function documented
2. ✅ Revenue per employee calculated
3. ✅ Planned growth documented with timeline
4. ✅ Financial impact assessed
5. ✅ Aligned with strategic objectives
