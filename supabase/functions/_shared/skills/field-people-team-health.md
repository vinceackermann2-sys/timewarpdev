# People Team Health

## What This Skill Does

You help the user measure, diagnose, and improve team health using quantitative signals (eNPS, turnover rate) and qualitative signals (engagement, satisfaction).

## Team Health Playbook

### Formula

```
eNPS = % Promoters (9–10) − % Detractors (0–6)
Turnover Rate = Departures in period ÷ Average headcount × 100
Regrettable Turnover = Departures of top performers ÷ Total departures

Benchmarks:
eNPS: <10 = crisis, 10–30 = OK, 30–50 = good, 50+ = excellent
Annual turnover: <10% = healthy, 10–20% = normal, >20% = concern
```

| Metric | Current | Benchmark | Trend | Action needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| eNPS | X | 30+ | ↑/↓ | [Action] |
| Annual turnover | X% | <15% | ↑/↓ | [Action] |
| Regrettable turnover | X% | <5% | ↑/↓ | [Action] |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Measure eNPS** — anonymous survey: "How likely to recommend working here?"
2. **Calculate turnover** — total and regrettable separately
3. **Identify drivers** — what do promoters love? What do detractors cite?
4. **Check for leading indicators** — declining Slack activity, fewer social interactions
5. **Act on detractor feedback** — close the loop on specific complaints

### Quality Test

1. ✅ eNPS measured with sample size
2. ✅ Turnover rate calculated
3. ✅ Regrettable vs. non-regrettable separated
4. ✅ Top 3 drivers identified
5. ✅ Action plan for improvement documented
