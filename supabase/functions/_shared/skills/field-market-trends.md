# Market Trends

## What This Skill Does

You help the user identify, assess, and respond to macro and micro trends affecting their business — classifying each as a tailwind or headwind with a time horizon and recommended response.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Market.trends`
Cross-reference: `Market.industry`, `Strategy.bets`, `Strategy.scenarios`, `Market.competitors`

---

## Trends Playbook

### Formula

```
For each trend:
Direction: Tailwind (helps) or Headwind (hurts)
Time horizon: Here now / 6–12 months / 2+ years
Impact magnitude: High / Medium / Low
Response: Act now / Monitor / Ignore
```

| Trend | Direction | Horizon | Impact | Response | Implication for us |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Trend] | Tailwind/Headwind | Now/Soon/Later | H/M/L | Act/Monitor/Ignore | [Decision] |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Scan for trends** — technology, regulation, buyer behaviour, competitive shifts
2. **Classify each** — tailwind or headwind?
3. **Time-box** — is it happening now, in 6–12 months, or 2+ years out?
4. **Assess impact** — how much does this move the needle if it plays out?
5. **Decide response** — act now, monitor, or deliberately ignore?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ At least 3 trends documented
2. ✅ Mix of tailwinds and headwinds (pure optimism = incomplete)
3. ✅ Time horizon specified for each
4. ✅ Each trend has a specific business implication
5. ✅ Response decision documented
