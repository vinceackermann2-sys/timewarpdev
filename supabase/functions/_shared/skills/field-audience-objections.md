# Audience Objections

## What This Skill Does

You help the user map every reason prospects don't buy or delay buying — and build specific counters for each, mapped to the buyer journey stage.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Audience.objections`
Cross-reference: `Audience.buyer`, `Audience.persona`, `Product.pricing`, `Product.social_proof`

## Objections Playbook

### Formula

```
Objection = [What they say] → [Real concern underneath] → [Counter with evidence]

Objections cluster by stage:
- Awareness: "I don't think I have this problem"
- Consideration: "I don't have the budget / I need to compare"
- Decision: "I need to check with my team / What if it doesn't work?"
- Post-purchase: "It's too complex / Not seeing value yet"
```

| Stage | What they say | Real concern | Counter | Evidence type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | [Quote] | [Fear] | [Response] | Social proof/data |
| Consideration | [Quote] | [Fear] | [Response] | ROI calc/case study |
| Decision | [Quote] | [Fear] | [Response] | Champion materials |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Collect from sales team** — what are the top 5 reasons deals stall or die?
2. **Decode the real concern** — "too expensive" usually means "I'm not confident in the ROI"
3. **Map to buyer stage** — when in the journey does this objection arise?
4. **Build specific counters** — not generic reassurance, but evidence (case study, ROI calc, guarantee)
5. **Create sales materials** — one-pager, battle card, or email template per objection

### Quality Test

1. ✅ At least 5 objections documented with real customer language
2. ✅ Each decoded to the real underlying concern
3. ✅ Mapped to buyer journey stage
4. ✅ Counter uses specific evidence (not "we're great")
5. ✅ Sales team has access to the counter materials
