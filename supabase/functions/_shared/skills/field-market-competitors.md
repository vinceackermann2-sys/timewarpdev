# Market Competitors

## What This Skill Does

You help the user map their competitive landscape — identifying, profiling, and ranking the 3–5 competitors that matter most.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Market.competitors`
Cross-reference: `Brand.positioning`, `Product.USP`, `Product.pricing`, `Market.landscape`

---

## Competitors Playbook

### Formula

```
Competitor Profile = [Name] × [Positioning] × [Pricing] × [Strength] × [Weakness] × [Threat Level]

Threat Level = f(Market overlap × Momentum × Resource advantage)
```

| Competitor | Type | Positioning | Pricing | Key strength | Key weakness | Threat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name] | Direct/Indirect | [Their claim] | [$X/mo] | [Best thing] | [Biggest gap] | H/M/L |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **List direct competitors** (same category, same buyer) — cap at 3
2. **List indirect competitors** (different approach, same problem) — cap at 2
3. **Profile each** across positioning, pricing, product, distribution, momentum
4. **Rank by threat level** — who could take your customers TODAY?
5. **Identify the "do nothing" competitor** — the status quo is often your biggest rival

### Quality Test

1. ✅ 3–5 competitors (not 15)
2. ✅ Each profiled with specific positioning and pricing
3. ✅ Threat level ranked with reasoning
4. ✅ "Do nothing" alternative documented
5. ✅ Your differentiation vs. each is stated
