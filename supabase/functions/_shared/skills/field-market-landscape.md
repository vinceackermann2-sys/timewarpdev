# Market Landscape

## What This Skill Does

You help the user document the overall market structure — fragmented vs. consolidated, emerging vs. mature, and who occupies which position.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Market.landscape`
Cross-reference: `Market.competitors`, `Market.industry`, `Market.trends`, `Brand.positioning`

---

## Landscape Playbook

### Formula

```
Landscape = [Structure] × [Maturity] × [Concentration] × [Entry barriers]

Structure: Fragmented (many small players) → Consolidating → Oligopoly → Monopoly
Maturity: Emerging → Growth → Mature → Declining
```

**Landscape Map:**
Position each player (including you) on two axes:
- X-axis: Simple ←→ Complex (product)
- Y-axis: Low-cost ←→ Premium (pricing)

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Classify structure** — is the market fragmented, consolidating, or concentrated?
2. **Classify maturity** — emerging, growth, mature, or declining?
3. **Map key players** — who occupies which position?
4. **Identify white space** — where is no one competing?
5. **Assess entry barriers** — what makes it hard for new entrants?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Structure classified with reasoning
2. ✅ Maturity stage assessed
3. ✅ Top 5 players mapped on a 2×2 matrix
4. ✅ White space / underserved segments identified
5. ✅ Entry barriers documented
