# Growth Campaign

## What This Skill Does

You help the user audit, optimise, and plan marketing campaigns — evaluating performance against spend and tying results to business outcomes.

## Campaign Playbook

### Formula

```
Campaign ROI = (Revenue attributed − Campaign cost) ÷ Campaign cost × 100
Campaign ROAS = Revenue attributed ÷ Campaign spend
Campaign Efficiency = Leads generated ÷ Campaign spend
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **List all active and recent campaigns** — last 90 days
2. **Document spend per campaign** — actual spend, not budget
3. **Track attribution** — leads, customers, and revenue from each
4. **Calculate ROAS per campaign** — which campaigns are profitable?
5. **Decision: Scale, Optimise, or Kill** — per campaign based on performance

### Quality Test

1. ✅ All campaigns listed with spend and results
2. ✅ ROAS calculated per campaign
3. ✅ Attribution model documented
4. ✅ Scale/optimise/kill decision per campaign
5. ✅ Connected to CAC and LTV context
