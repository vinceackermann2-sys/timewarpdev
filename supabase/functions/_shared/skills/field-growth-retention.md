# Growth Retention

## What This Skill Does

You help the user measure, diagnose, and improve retention — the growth multiplier that determines whether acquisition spending compounds or leaks.

## Retention Playbook

### Formula

```
Day N Retention = Users active on Day N ÷ Users who signed up on Day 0
Monthly Retention = Active users this month ÷ Active users last month

Retention is the growth multiplier:
95% monthly retention → doubles faster than 85% retention regardless of acquisition
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Measure cohort retention** — D1, D7, D30, D90
2. **Define "active"** — what action counts as retained?
3. **Identify the churn cliff** — where does the biggest drop happen?
4. **Analyse by segment** — does retention differ by channel, plan, or cohort?
5. **Find the activation action** — what do retained users do that churned users don't?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ D7, D30, D90 retention measured
2. ✅ "Active" clearly defined
3. ✅ Churn cliff identified
4. ✅ Segmented by at least one dimension
