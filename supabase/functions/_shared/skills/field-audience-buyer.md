# Audience Buyer Type

## What This Skill Does

You help the user map the buying committee — identifying the economic buyer, champion, end-user, and blockers involved in the purchase decision.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Audience.buyer`
Cross-reference: `Audience.persona`, `Audience.objections`, `Product.pricing`, `Product.USP`

---

## Buyer Type Playbook

### Formula

```
Buying Committee = Economic Buyer + Champion + End User + [Blocker]

Economic Buyer: Signs the contract / approves the budget
Champion: Internal advocate who pushes for purchase
End User: Person who uses the product daily
Blocker: Person who can veto (IT, legal, procurement)
```

| Role | Title | Cares about | Objection risk | How to win them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Economic Buyer | [Title] | ROI, risk | Budget, timing | Business case + proof |
| Champion | [Title] | Solving their problem | Political capital | Quick wins + enablement |
| End User | [Title] | Ease of use | Learning curve | Demo + trial |
| Blocker | [Title] | Compliance, security | Integration risk | Documentation + audit |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Map all roles** involved in the purchase decision
2. **Identify who signs** — the economic buyer is not always the end user
3. **Identify who champions** — who internally pushes for your product?
4. **Identify potential blockers** — IT, legal, procurement, the incumbent vendor's champion
5. **For each, document: what they care about + what their objection will be**

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Economic buyer identified with title
2. ✅ Champion identified (may be the same as end user)
3. ✅ Potential blockers named
4. ✅ Each role has specific concerns and objections
5. ✅ Win strategy documented per role
