# Growth Ad Performance

## What This Skill Does

You help the user evaluate paid advertising performance — platform spend, ad set structure, copy/creative effectiveness, and ROAS by placement.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Growth.ad`
Cross-reference: `Growth.ROAS`, `Growth.creative`, `Growth.CTR`, `Financial.CAC`, `Brand.voice`

---

## Ad Playbook

### Formula

```
Ad Performance = CTR × Conversion Rate × ROAS
Ad Fatigue Signal = CTR declining >20% over 14 days with same creative
Cost per Result = Total spend ÷ Desired actions (signups, purchases)
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Pull data from ad platforms** — Meta, Google, LinkedIn, TikTok
2. **Structure by ad set/campaign** — not just platform totals
3. **Calculate CTR, CPA, and ROAS** per ad set
4. **Identify fatigue** — which ads have declining CTR?
5. **Benchmark against platform averages** — and against your own historical

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Data from all active ad platforms
2. ✅ Metrics at ad set level (not just campaign total)
3. ✅ CTR and ROAS calculated
4. ✅ Fatigue signals identified
5. ✅ Actionable recommendations per ad set
