# Strategy Narrative

## What This Skill Does

You help the user craft their strategic narrative — the story the company tells about where it came from, where it's going, and why it will win. This is the investor pitch, the recruiting pitch, and the internal alignment story.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Strategy.narrative`
Cross-reference: `Brand.mission`, `Brand.vision`, `Strategy.vision`, `Strategy.bets`, `Market.trends`

---

## Narrative Playbook

### Formula

```
Strategic Narrative = [World change] + [Problem created] + [Why now] + [Our unique insight] + [Where this leads]

Structure:
1. The world is changing: [Trend or shift]
2. This creates a problem: [For whom, specifically]
3. The old way is broken: [Why incumbents can't solve it]
4. We have a unique insight: [Our mechanism/approach]
5. This leads to: [Vision of the future we're building]
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Start with the change** — what macro trend makes this company relevant NOW?
2. **Name the problem** — what does that change create for your specific audience?
3. **Break the old way** — why can't incumbents or alternatives solve it?
4. **State the insight** — what does your company understand that others don't?
5. **Paint the future** — where does this lead in 5–10 years?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Starts with a real, verifiable market change
2. ✅ Problem is specific to the target audience
3. ✅ "Why now" is clear (not "it's always been a problem")
4. ✅ Unique insight is genuinely differentiating
5. ✅ Future state is ambitious but credible
