# Audience Testimonials

## What This Skill Does

You help the user collect, curate, and deploy customer testimonials — prioritising specific outcome quotes over generic praise, and mapping them to buyer journey stages.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Audience.testimonials`
Cross-reference: `Product.social_proof`, `Audience.NPS`, `Brand.reputation`, `Audience.persona`

---

## Testimonials Playbook

### Formula

```
Strong testimonial = [Named person] + [Specific result] + [Before/After contrast]

Persuasion hierarchy:
1. Outcome quote: "We reduced churn from 8% to 3.2% in 90 days" — [Name, Role, Company]
2. Transformation quote: "Before X we spent 3hrs/day on Y, now it takes 10 minutes"
3. Endorsement quote: "The best tool we've ever used for X"
4. Sentiment quote: "Love it!" (weakest — avoid relying on these)
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Mine existing sources** — NPS surveys, support tickets, social mentions, G2 reviews
2. **Ask the right question** — "What specific result have you achieved?" not "Do you like our product?"
3. **Get permission** — name, title, company, and a photo if possible
4. **Classify by tier** — outcome > transformation > endorsement > sentiment
5. **Map to journey stage** — which testimonial best serves awareness vs. decision vs. onboarding?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ At least 3 testimonials with real names and companies
2. ✅ At least 1 specific outcome with a number
3. ✅ Testimonials cover different personas or use cases
4. ✅ Mapped to buyer journey stages
5. ✅ Current (< 12 months old)
