# Audience NPS

## What This Skill Does

You help the user measure, interpret, and act on their Net Promoter Score — understanding what drives promoters vs. detractors and using NPS as a leading indicator for retention and growth.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Audience.NPS`
Cross-reference: `Financial.churn`, `Product.social_proof`, `Brand.reputation`, `Growth.retention`

---

## NPS Playbook

### Formula

```
NPS = % Promoters (9–10) − % Detractors (0–6)

Range: −100 to +100
Benchmarks: Below 0 = crisis, 0–30 = OK, 30–50 = good, 50–70 = excellent, 70+ = world-class
```

**NPS Action Matrix:**

| Score range | Interpretation | Priority action |
|---|---|---|
| < 0 | Product or service crisis | Fix the top detractor complaint immediately |
| 0–30 | Passable but fragile | Investigate what separates promoters from passives |
| 30–50 | Healthy | Activate promoters for referrals and testimonials |
| 50+ | Strong competitive advantage | Systematise promoter advocacy at scale |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Survey** — "How likely are you to recommend us?" (0–10) + "Why?" (open text)
2. **Calculate** — %Promoters − %Detractors
3. **Segment** — NPS by cohort, plan, segment, or acquisition channel
4. **Analyse verbatims** — what do promoters love? What do detractors hate?
5. **Act** — close the loop with detractors (outreach), activate promoters (referral, testimonials)

### Quality Test

1. ✅ NPS score documented with sample size
2. ✅ Response rate > 20% for statistical validity
3. ✅ Segmented by at least one dimension (plan, cohort, channel)
4. ✅ Top 3 promoter drivers documented
5. ✅ Top 3 detractor complaints documented with action plans
