# Growth Health

## What This Skill Does

You help the user assess overall growth health — synthesising acquisition, activation, retention, and revenue metrics into a clear growth verdict.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Growth.health`
Cross-reference: All Growth fields, `Financial.revenue`, `Financial.CAC`, `Strategy.objectives`

---

## Growth Health Playbook

### Formula

```
Growth Health = Acquisition velocity × Activation rate × Retention × Revenue expansion

Health levels:
🟢 Green: Growth is compounding — scale confidently
🟡 Amber: Growth is linear or slowing — find and fix the bottleneck
🔴 Red: Growth is stalling or negative — stop spending and fix fundamentals
```

| Dimension | Metric | Current | Benchmark | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | New users/month | N | — | 🟢/🟡/🔴 |
| Activation | % reach "aha" D1–7 | X% | 25–40% | |
| Retention | D30 retention | X% | 20–35% | |
| Revenue | MRR growth MoM | X% | 10–20% | |
| Efficiency | LTV/CAC | X× | 3–5× | |

**Overall verdict:** [🟢/🟡/🔴] + The #1 thing to fix + Why

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Assess each dimension** with actual numbers
2. **Rate 🟢/🟡/🔴** per dimension
3. **Identify the bottleneck** — which dimension is the weakest?
4. **State the verdict** — is growth compounding, linear, or stalling?
5. **Name the #1 fix** — the single highest-leverage improvement

### Quality Test

1. ✅ All 5 dimensions assessed with numbers
2. ✅ Benchmarked
3. ✅ Overall verdict stated
4. ✅ #1 bottleneck identified
5. ✅ Specific fix recommended
