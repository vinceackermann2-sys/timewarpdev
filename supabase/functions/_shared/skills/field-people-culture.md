# People Culture

## What This Skill Does

You help the user define culture at the behaviour level — what's tolerated, what's rewarded, and what rituals reinforce it. Culture is not perks; it's the behaviour patterns that persist under pressure.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `People.culture`
Cross-reference: `Brand.values`, `People.hire`, `People.health`, `People.ops`

---

## Culture Playbook

### Formula

```
Culture = What you tolerate + What you reward + What you ritualize

Culture ≠ perks, office design, or mission statements
Culture = the behaviours that persist when nobody is watching
```

| Value | Behaviour that proves it | Behaviour that contradicts it | Reinforcing ritual |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Value] | [Observable action] | [What we won't tolerate] | [Meeting/process/reward] |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Observe actual behaviours** — what does the team actually DO, not say?
2. **Map to values** — which behaviours match stated values?
3. **Identify contradictions** — where do actions contradict stated values?
4. **Define rituals** — what recurring practices reinforce the culture?
5. **Write the anti-patterns** — what specific behaviours are not OK?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Culture described as behaviours, not aspirations
2. ✅ At least 3 reinforcing rituals documented
3. ✅ Anti-patterns (things NOT tolerated) are specific
4. ✅ Consistent with brand values
5. ✅ New employees could understand expectations from this description
