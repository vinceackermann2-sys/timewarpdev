# Audience Journey

## What This Skill Does

You help the user map the complete buyer journey — from first awareness through purchase to advocacy — with conversion metrics, content needs, and friction points at each stage.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Audience.journey`
Cross-reference: `Growth.funnel`, `Audience.triggers`, `Audience.objections`, `Growth.channel`

---

## Journey Playbook

### Formula

```
Journey = Awareness → Consideration → Decision → Onboarding → Retention → Advocacy

Each stage: [Entry trigger] → [Questions they ask] → [Content they need] → [Exit action] → [Conversion rate]
```

| Stage | Entry trigger | Customer question | Content needed | Key metric | Friction point |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | [Trigger event] | "Do I have this problem?" | Blog, social, ads | Impressions → clicks | [Blocker] |
| Consideration | [Clicked/engaged] | "What are my options?" | Comparison, case studies | Clicks → signups | [Blocker] |
| Decision | [Trial/demo] | "Is this the right choice?" | Proof, pricing, ROI | Signups → paid | [Blocker] |
| Onboarding | [Purchased] | "How do I get value?" | Guides, quick wins | Paid → activated | [Blocker] |
| Retention | [Activated] | "Should I stay?" | Advanced features, support | Activated → retained | [Blocker] |
| Advocacy | [Retained 90d+] | "Should I recommend this?" | Referral program, community | Retained → referral | [Blocker] |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Map all stages** from first touch to advocacy
2. **For each stage, document the customer question** — what are they trying to answer?
3. **Identify the content need** — what helps them answer that question?
4. **Add conversion metrics** — what % move to the next stage?
5. **Find the friction points** — where is the biggest drop-off?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ All 6 stages mapped
2. ✅ Each has a specific customer question
3. ✅ Conversion rates documented (even estimated)
4. ✅ Biggest friction point identified
5. ✅ Content gaps identified per stage
