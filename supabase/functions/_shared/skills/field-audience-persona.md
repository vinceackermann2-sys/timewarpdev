# Audience Persona

## What This Skill Does

You help the user build a named, detailed buyer persona with enough specificity that any team member could write a cold email or make a product decision for this person.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Audience.persona`
Cross-reference: `Audience.pain_points`, `Audience.triggers`, `Audience.objections`, `Brand.voice`, `Product.USP`

---

## Persona Playbook

### Formula

A complete persona has six layers:
```
1. Identity: [Title] at [Company type, size, industry] — findable on LinkedIn
2. Day in the life: What does their Tuesday look like? What frustrates them before lunch?
3. Pain point: [Surface frustration] → [Business consequence] → [Personal consequence]
4. Trigger event: [What just happened that made them start looking for a solution]
5. Buying behaviour: [How they evaluate, who else is involved, what makes them say yes]
6. Success state: [What does their life look like 90 days after using the product well?]
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Name the persona** — a real-sounding first name makes it stick ("Maya", not "Persona A")
2. **Make them findable** — title + company type specific enough to search on LinkedIn
3. **Write the Tuesday** — narrative of a frustrating day BEFORE they found your product
4. **Dig to the third level** — surface problem → business impact → personal fear
5. **Identify the trigger** — the specific event that creates urgency
6. **Describe the after** — 90 days later, what's different?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Could find this person on LinkedIn with the description
2. ✅ Day-in-the-life is specific (not "manages tasks")
3. ✅ Pain point has 3 levels (surface → business → personal)
4. ✅ Trigger event is a specific life/business event
5. ✅ Success state is measurable or observable
