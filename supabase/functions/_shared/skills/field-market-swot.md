# Market SWOT

## What This Skill Does

You help the user build a decision-grade SWOT analysis where every entry drives a specific action — not a generic brainstorm.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Market.SWOT`
Cross-reference: `Market.competitors`, `Market.trends`, `Product.USP`, `Financial.revenue`, `Strategy.objectives`

## SWOT Playbook

### Formula

```
Decision-Grade SWOT: Every entry must answer "So what?" with an implied action.

| Category | Entry | Evidence | So what? (Decision implication) |
```

| Category | Entry | Evidence | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strength | [Specific advantage] | [Data/proof] | [Double down on...] |
| Weakness | [Specific gap] | [Data/proof] | [Fix by...] |
| Opportunity | [Specific opening] | [Trend/signal] | [Capture by...] |
| Threat | [Specific risk] | [Competitor/market signal] | [Defend by...] |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Strengths** — what does the data say you're genuinely good at? (Not what you hope)
2. **Weaknesses** — what do customers and the team actually complain about?
3. **Opportunities** — what market shifts create openings?
4. **Threats** — what competitor moves or market changes could hurt you?
5. **For EVERY entry: add the "So what?"** — the implied decision

### Quality Test

1. ✅ 3–5 entries per quadrant (not 15)
2. ✅ Every entry has evidence/source
3. ✅ Every entry has an action implication
4. ✅ Strengths are evidence-based, not aspirational
5. ✅ Threats include at least one competitive and one market threat
