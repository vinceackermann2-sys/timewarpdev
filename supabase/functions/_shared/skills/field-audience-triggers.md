# Audience Triggers

## What This Skill Does

You help the user identify the specific life or business events that create urgency to buy NOW. Without a trigger, there's interest but no urgency.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Audience.triggers`
Cross-reference: `Audience.persona`, `Audience.pain_points`, `Growth.channel`, `Growth.campaign`

---

## Triggers Playbook

### Formula

```
Trigger = [Event] + [Consequence if not addressed] + [Time pressure]

Types:
- Life trigger: New role, promotion, company scale event, failed audit
- Business trigger: Missed target, new competitor, product launch, investor pressure
- Seasonal trigger: Budget cycle, annual planning, regulatory deadline
- Social trigger: Peer recommendation, seeing competitor use the product
```

| Trigger event | Type | Consequence | Time pressure | Marketing response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Event] | Life/Business/Seasonal/Social | [What happens if ignored] | [Deadline] | [How to be present when it fires] |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Ask existing customers** — "What was happening when you started looking for a solution?"
2. **Classify by type** — life, business, seasonal, or social
3. **Add consequence** — what happens if the trigger is ignored?
4. **Map the marketing response** — how do you reach people when this trigger fires?
5. **Rank by frequency** — which triggers generate the most pipeline?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ At least 3 triggers documented with specific events (not "they need our product")
2. ✅ Each classified by type
3. ✅ Time pressure documented (deadline or window)
4. ✅ Marketing channel mapped per trigger
5. ✅ Validated from real customer conversations
