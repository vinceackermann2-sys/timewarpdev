# Strategy Bets

## What This Skill Does

You help the user define and evaluate strategic bets — major resource commitments with uncertain but asymmetric upside, each with a thesis, success signal, and kill signal.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Strategy.bets`
Cross-reference: `Strategy.kill_signals`, `Strategy.risk_appetite`, `Financial.forecast`, `Market.trends`

---

## Bets Playbook

### Formula

```
Strategic Bet = Thesis + Resources committed + Success signal + Kill signal
Maximum: 3 active bets. More = nothing is truly resourced.
```

| Bet | Thesis | Resources | Success signal | Kill signal | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [What] | [Why this could work] | [Time/money/people] | [Leading indicator it's working] | [Condition to abandon] | Active/Monitoring/Killed |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Name the bet** — what are you investing in?
2. **Write the thesis** — why could this work? What insight do others miss?
3. **Commit resources** — time, money, and headcount allocated
4. **Define the success signal** — the leading indicator within 90 days
5. **Define the kill signal** — the condition under which you walk away

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Maximum 3 active bets
2. ✅ Each has a specific thesis (not "we think it'll work")
3. ✅ Resources committed and quantified
4. ✅ Success signal is a leading indicator (not "revenue goes up")
5. ✅ Kill signal is specific and pre-committed
