# Financial Revenue

## What This Skill Does

You help the user map, analyse, and optimise their revenue architecture — identifying all streams, growth rates, margin profiles, and concentration risks.

## Revenue Playbook

### Formula

```
Revenue Architecture:
MRR = Sum of all monthly recurring subscription revenue
ARR = MRR × 12
Net Revenue = Gross Revenue − Refunds − Discounts
Revenue Concentration Risk = % from top 10 customers (>30% = high risk)
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **List every revenue stream** — subscription, expansion, services, one-time
2. **Classify by type** — recurring, non-recurring, usage-based
3. **Calculate growth rate** — MoM and YoY for each stream
4. **Check concentration** — what % comes from your top 10 customers?
5. **Note margin per stream** — services revenue often has lower margins

### Quality Test

1. ✅ All revenue streams documented with monthly/annual figures
2. ✅ Recurring vs. non-recurring classified
3. ✅ Growth rate by stream documented
4. ✅ Concentration risk assessed
5. ✅ Total MRR/ARR calculated
