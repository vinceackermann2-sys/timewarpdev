# People Org Chart

## What This Skill Does

You help the user design or refine their org structure — ensuring it follows strategy, minimises communication overhead, and eliminates single points of failure.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `People.org_chart`
Cross-reference: `People.employee`, `People.headcount`, `Strategy.objectives`

---

## Org Chart Playbook

### Formula

```
Org design follows strategy, not convention.

Questions before drawing a chart:
1. What outcomes must the team produce? (from Strategy.objectives)
2. What capabilities are required?
3. Where are single points of failure?
4. What's the communication overhead?
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Map current structure** — who reports to whom?
2. **Identify design mistakes** — managers without reports, ICs reporting to CEO at scale
3. **Check span of control** — optimal: 5–8 direct reports
4. **Verify ownership** — is every key outcome owned by exactly one person?
5. **Plan for next stage** — what changes at 2× headcount?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Complete reporting structure documented
2. ✅ Every key outcome has a clear owner
3. ✅ Span of control within 5–8 per manager
4. ✅ No single points of failure (bus factor > 1 for critical functions)
5. ✅ Structure supports stated strategy
