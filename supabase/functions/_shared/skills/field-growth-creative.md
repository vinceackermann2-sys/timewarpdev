# Growth Creative

## What This Skill Does

You help the user audit and improve ad creative — hooks, angles, CTAs, and fatigue management.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Growth.creative`
Cross-reference: `Growth.CTR`, `Growth.ad`, `Brand.voice`, `Brand.tone`, `Audience.pain_points`

---

## Creative Playbook

### Formula

```
Creative Performance = Hook strength (CTR) × Angle relevance (Conversion) × CTA clarity (Action rate)
Creative Fatigue = CTR declining >20% over 14 days on same audience
```

| Creative | Hook type | Angle | CTA | CTR | Age (days) | Fatigue? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Creative] | Pain/Benefit/Curiosity/Social proof | [Specific angle] | [CTA text] | X% | N | Y/N |

**Creative angles:**
- Pain: "Tired of [frustration]?"
- Benefit: "[Specific outcome] in [timeframe]"
- Curiosity: "The [approach] that [surprising result]"
- Social proof: "[Customer] achieved [result]"
- Contrarian: "Why [common belief] is wrong"

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Inventory all active creative** — every ad in rotation
2. **Classify hook and angle** — what grabs attention and what's the message?
3. **Track CTR and age** — identify fatigue
4. **Always have 3 concepts testing** — rotate losers, scale winners
5. **Match to brand voice** — creative must sound like the brand

### Quality Test

1. ✅ All active creative inventoried
2. ✅ Hook type and angle classified
3. ✅ CTR and fatigue assessed
4. ✅ At least 3 new concepts in testing
5. ✅ Creative aligned with brand voice
