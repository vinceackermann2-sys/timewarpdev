# Audience Pain Points

## What This Skill Does

You help the user map specific, named customer frustrations — digging past surface complaints to the business and personal consequences that drive buying decisions.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Audience.pain_points`
Cross-reference: `Product.features`, `Product.benefits`, `Audience.persona`, `Audience.triggers`

---

## Pain Points Playbook

### Formula

```
[Surface frustration] which means [business consequence] which means [personal consequence].

Level 1 (Surface): "Our dashboard is slow"
Level 2 (Business): "I can't present accurate pipeline data to the board"
Level 3 (Personal): "I look incompetent, which puts my job at risk"
```

Buying decisions are made at Level 3. Always dig to the third level.

| Pain point | Level 1 (Surface) | Level 2 (Business) | Level 3 (Personal) | Product feature that resolves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| #1 | [Frustration] | [Impact] | [Fear/risk] | [Feature] |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **List all known frustrations** — from sales calls, support tickets, reviews, surveys
2. **For each, ask "which means…" twice** — get to the 3rd level
3. **Rank by frequency** — which pain points appear most often?
4. **Map to features** — does each pain have a product feature that resolves it?
5. **Identify unaddressed pains** — any Level 3 consequences with no product solution?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ At least 5 pain points documented
2. ✅ Each has all three levels (surface → business → personal)
3. ✅ Ranked by frequency/severity
4. ✅ Mapped to product features
5. ✅ Sourced from real customer data (not assumptions)
