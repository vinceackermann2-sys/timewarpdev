# Growth Email

## What This Skill Does

You help the user audit and improve email marketing performance — open rates, click rates, deliverability, list health, and sequence effectiveness.

## Email Playbook

### Formula

```
Email Health = Deliverability × Open Rate × Click Rate × Conversion Rate
List Health = Active subscribers ÷ Total subscribers (healthy: >70%)
Revenue per Email = Total email revenue ÷ Emails sent
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Pull email metrics** — deliverability, opens, clicks, unsubscribes
2. **Assess list health** — what % of subscribers are active?
3. **Audit sequences** — what automated sequences exist? What's their performance?
4. **Check segmentation** — are emails targeted or blast-to-all?
5. **Identify the highest-performing email** — what made it work?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Core metrics documented (deliverability, open, click)
2. ✅ List health assessed
3. ✅ Email sequences documented with performance
4. ✅ Segmentation approach described
5. ✅ Improvement actions identified
