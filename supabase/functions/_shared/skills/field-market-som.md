# Market SOM

## What This Skill Does

You help the user estimate their Serviceable Obtainable Market — the realistic revenue they can capture in 1–3 years given competitive dynamics, resources, and execution capacity.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Market.SOM`
Cross-reference: `Market.TAM`, `Market.SAM`, `Market.competitors`, `Financial.revenue`, `Strategy.objectives`

---

## SOM Playbook

### Formula

```
SOM = SAM × Realistic market share capture (1–3 year horizon)

Benchmarks:
- Without strong network effects: SOM rarely > 10–20% of SAM
- With network effects: SOM can be 30–50% in concentrated markets
- New entrant: SOM year 1 is typically 1–5% of SAM
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Start from SAM** — the reachable market
2. **Estimate market share** — based on competitive position, not aspiration
3. **Validate against current revenue** — SOM should be achievable from current trajectory
4. **Factor in competitive dynamics** — how many viable competitors share the SAM?
5. **Time-bound it** — SOM in 1 year, 2 years, 3 years

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Derived from SAM with visible math
2. ✅ Market share assumption is realistic (not "we'll get 50%")
3. ✅ Competitive dynamics factored in
4. ✅ Time-bounded (1–3 year horizon)
5. ✅ Consistent with financial projections and growth rate
