# Audience Segment

## What This Skill Does

You help the user define distinct sub-groups within their audience that have meaningfully different needs, buying behaviour, or value potential.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Audience.segment`
Cross-reference: `Audience.persona`, `Product.pricing`, `Financial.revenue`, `Financial.LTV`

---

## Segment Playbook

### Formula

```
Segment = [Group Name] × [Defining Characteristic] × [Different Need] × [Revenue Potential]

A valid segment must have all four: nameable, distinguishable, sizable, and reachable.
```

| Segment | Defining characteristic | Primary need | Revenue potential | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name] | [What makes them different] | [What they need most] | [High/Medium/Low] | [Primary/Secondary/Avoid] |

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Examine existing customers** — where do natural clusters form?
2. **Look for behavioural differences** — do some customers use the product differently?
3. **Check value differences** — do some segments have higher LTV?
4. **Name 2–4 segments** — primary, secondary, and segments to avoid
5. **For each: one sentence on why they're different**

### Quality Test

1. ✅ 2–4 named segments (not 10)
2. ✅ Each has a defining characteristic that creates a different need
3. ✅ Revenue potential estimated per segment
4. ✅ Primary segment identified
5. ✅ "Avoid" segment named (customers who are a bad fit)
