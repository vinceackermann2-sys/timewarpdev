# Market SAM

## What This Skill Does

You help the user calculate their Serviceable Addressable Market — the portion of TAM reachable with their current GTM motion, geography, and product capabilities.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Market.SAM`
Cross-reference: `Market.TAM`, `Market.SOM`, `Audience.segment`, `Growth.channel`, `Product.features`

---

## SAM Playbook

### Formula

```
SAM = TAM × [Geographic filter] × [Segment filter] × [Product capability filter]

SAM answers: "Of the total market, which companies could we actually sell to with our current model?"
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Start from TAM** — take the documented total
2. **Apply geographic filter** — which regions/countries do you operate in?
3. **Apply segment filter** — which company sizes/industries can you serve today?
4. **Apply product filter** — which use cases does your product actually support?
5. **Document each filter with a %** — show the math

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Derived from documented TAM with visible math
2. ✅ Each filter has a specific percentage and rationale
3. ✅ SAM < TAM (always)
4. ✅ Reflects CURRENT capabilities, not future aspirations
5. ✅ Consistent with where current customers actually come from
