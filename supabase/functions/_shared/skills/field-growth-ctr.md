# Growth CTR

## What This Skill Does

You help the user measure, benchmark, and improve click-through rates across channels, placements, and creative — treating CTR as a signal of message-market fit.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Growth.CTR`
Cross-reference: `Growth.ad`, `Growth.creative`, `Growth.email`, `Brand.voice`

---

## CTR Playbook

### Formula

```
CTR = Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100

Benchmarks by context:
- Cold paid ads: >1% = good
- Warm retargeting: >2% = good
- Email: >2.5% = good
- Hot list/existing customers: >5% = good
```

| Channel/Placement | Impressions | Clicks | CTR | Benchmark | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Channel] | N | N | X% | X% | ±X% |

**CTR is a proxy for message-market fit.** Low CTR = wrong message or wrong audience. High CTR + low conversion = right click, wrong landing page.

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Pull CTR by channel** — paid, email, organic, each separately
2. **Break down by placement** — feed vs. stories vs. search
3. **Benchmark** — compare to channel-specific standards
4. **Correlate with conversion** — high CTR without conversion is misleading
5. **Identify winners** — which creative/channel has highest CTR? Why?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ CTR measured by channel and placement
2. ✅ Benchmarked against channel-specific standards
3. ✅ Correlated with downstream conversion
4. ✅ Top-performing creative identified
5. ✅ Improvement hypothesis documented
