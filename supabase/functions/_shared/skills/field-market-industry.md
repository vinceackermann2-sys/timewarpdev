# Market Industry

## What This Skill Does

You help the user define and document the industry or market category their business operates in — including how the category is evolving and where boundaries are shifting.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Market.industry`
Cross-reference: `Market.trends`, `Market.landscape`, `Brand.positioning`, `Product.USP`

---

## Industry Playbook

### Formula

```
Industry Definition = [Category name] × [Scope boundaries] × [Lifecycle stage] × [Key dynamics]

Lifecycle stages: Emerging → Growth → Mature → Declining
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Name the category** — what shelf does this business sit on?
2. **Define boundaries** — what's included and excluded?
3. **Assess lifecycle stage** — emerging, growth, mature, or declining?
4. **Identify key dynamics** — consolidation, fragmentation, regulation, technology shifts?
5. **Note category creation potential** — is the company defining a new category?

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Category named clearly
2. ✅ Lifecycle stage assessed
3. ✅ 2–3 key dynamics identified
4. ✅ Adjacent categories noted (where customers come from and go to)
5. ✅ Category creation opportunity assessed
