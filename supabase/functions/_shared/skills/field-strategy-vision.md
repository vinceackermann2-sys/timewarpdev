# Strategy Vision

## What This Skill Does

You help the user define or refine their strategic vision — the future state the company is building toward, distinct from the brand vision by being operationally actionable and time-bound.

## Business DNA Context

Primary field: `Strategy.vision`
Cross-reference: `Brand.vision`, `Strategy.objectives`, `Strategy.milestones`, `Market.TAM`

---

## Vision Playbook

### Formula

```
Strategic Vision = [What changes] + [For whom] + [By when] + [At what scale]

Distinct from Brand Vision: Brand vision is aspirational; strategic vision is executable.
The strategic vision should decompose into objectives and milestones.
```

### How to Fill This Field

1. **Define the 3–5 year destination** — what does success look like?
2. **Make it measurable** — include a scale or time marker
3. **Ensure it constrains** — what are you NOT building?
4. **Connect to objectives** — can you derive OKRs from this vision?
5. **Align with brand vision** — strategic vision is the execution path to brand vision

### Quality Test

1. ✅ Time-bounded (3–5 year horizon)
2. ✅ Measurable or verifiable
3. ✅ Constrains scope (says what you're NOT doing)
4. ✅ Decomposable into objectives
5. ✅ Consistent with brand vision
